About me

Where I studied and what I like to build.

Education and background

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Industrial Information Technology from INES-Ruhengeri in Rwanda. The programme gave me a base in programming, electronics, databases, networking and industrial systems.

My final-year dissertation was an IoT-based crash detection system with automatic emergency notification, which combined hardware prototyping, firmware, a database and a web dashboard.

Technical approach

How I move from a real-world problem to a working system.

    Start with the failureI define what the system must detect, and what a false alarm or a missed event would cost.
    Separate sensing from decidingRaw readings are filtered and compared to thresholds before any alert is sent.
    Design for the person receiving the alertA message has to say what happened, where, and how serious it is.
